# Vexlio proposed brand — clearance hold

Checked: 2026-11-03

**Decision: REJECT for Northstar rebrand. Do not publish or configure as merchant or sender name.**

## Existing commercial use
- https://vexlio.com/ is an active interactive diagramming and technical-documentation software service, with subscriptions and business customers.
- https://vexlio.com/legal/ identifies **VEXLIO LLC** as publisher and asserts intellectual-property and brand rights (copyright notice dated 2025).
- https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/vexlio identifies Vexlio LLC as an active US software company.
- `vexlio.com` is occupied and actively serving the business.

## USPTO / legal limitations
- An exact VEXLIO federal trademark registration was **not verified** using accessible search results; absence of a surfaced result does not mean the name is free.
- USPTO opposition 91291816 concerns **VEXLO**, a different mark, and is not a determination about Vexlio.
- Existing commercial use can give rise to common-law rights; do not proceed without professional clearance. Because an exact-name software company already exists, choose another name.

## Branch status
Existing Northstar branding is deliberately retained in storefront, Stripe-facing copy, Resend emails, and docs. Do not merge or deploy this branch without explicit owner approval. SMB service catalog and terms development can proceed independently of naming.
